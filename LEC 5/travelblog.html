Explore Rajasthan

Royal Palaces • Golden Deserts • Beautiful Lakes

Jaipur - The Pink City

Jaipur, known as the Pink City, is one of Rajasthan's most famous destinations. Explore the stunning Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, City Palace and vibrant local markets that showcase Rajasthan's rich culture and heritage.

Read More

Udaipur - The City of Lakes

Udaipur is famous for its beautiful lakes and magnificent palaces. A boat ride on Lake Pichola and a visit to City Palace make Udaipur one of the most romantic cities in India.

Read More

Jaisalmer - The Golden City

Located in the heart of the Thar Desert, Jaisalmer is known for its golden sandstone architecture, desert safaris and historic forts. It offers an unforgettable desert experience.
